OFFICIAL GUIDANCE & SUPPORT

Need Help Applying?

Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu

For official scheme verification, UDID card registration, assistive device camps, and application submission, contact your local District Differently Abled Welfare Office (DDAWO) or visit a nearby TN e-Sevai / Common Service Centre (CSC).

  • State Commissionerate: Commissionerate for Welfare of the Differently Abled, No. 5, Kamarajar Salai, Lady Willingdon College Campus, Chennai – 600 005.
  • District Level: District Differently Abled Welfare Officer (DDAWO) located at every District Collectorate in Tamil Nadu.
  • Official Portals: scd.tn.gov.in • swavlambancard.gov.in (UDID) • tnega.tn.gov.in (e-Sevai)
ℹ️ Note: Please verify current office timings and official helpline numbers directly via the Tamil Nadu Government directory before travelling.
